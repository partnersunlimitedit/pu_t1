About Us

A network built on people

Like-minded mid-size logistics providers, connected across the world by shared values, mutual protection and a common ambition to grow.

Who We Are

Partners across the world

We are a group of like-minded mid-size logistics providers, forming a network of partners across the world. Our strength lies in the quality of our people and the trust between our members.

Active members of the Association are private enterprises or corporations involved in the freight forwarding industry, actively supporting the purpose and objectives of the Association.

What We Believe — Our Core Value

PEOPLE · PEOPLE · PEOPLE

We believe the most important element is the PEOPLE — our members. We subscribe to:

  • All members are accepted and treated equally, regardless of market and company size.
  • The network is about connecting people and connecting needs.
  • The network must create value for its members.
  • The best ideas on how to operate come from its members.
