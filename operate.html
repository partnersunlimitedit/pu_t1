How We Operate

A global network, regionally run

For better management of growth, the network is subdivided into four regions — combining local expertise with global coordination under a member-elected Board.

Four Global Regions

Local expertise, global reach

The Americas

North, Central & South America.

Asia – Pacific

East Asia, SE Asia & Oceania.

Africa – Mideast

MEA and the Gulf region.

The EU

Europe and the European Union.

Governance

Led by members, for members

Qualified members are invited to the Advisory Board to represent the views and interests of the membership. These members form the Management Board of Directors, each holding the position of Regional Director for one of the four regions.

  • Advisory Board — represents members' views and interests.
  • Board of Directors — governs and approves membership applications.
  • Regional Directors — one per region, drawn from active members.

Membership in Practice

01
Apply

Applicants meeting the standards may apply in writing or online to the Chairman. The Board may reject an application without comments.

02
Represent

Each member represents the Association within their assigned market of residence and business.

03
Resign

Members may resign with three months' notice, by end of month. Annual fees remain due — no refund. Members may only promote offices assigned within the network.

Membership Standards

Grounds for disqualification

To protect every member, the network upholds strict standards of conduct and performance.

Conduct

Conduct that would disgrace or act against the network.

Performance

Consecutively below performance expectations for 3 years.

Criminal Offence

Conviction of a criminal offence.

Unpaid Dues

Failure to settle the annual membership dues.

Member Payments

Failure to settle payments owed to other members.
