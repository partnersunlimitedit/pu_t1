Members Evaluation

Excellence, measured

An objective peer evaluation program, a strict code of ethics, and a debt protection fund — the three pillars that keep our network strong and trustworthy.

Evaluation Program (EP)

Annual Members Evaluation

All members are objectively evaluated by their peers across five categories, each carrying a 10-point value. Members must maintain a minimum score of 50% efficiency.

Members who do not maintain the minimum score for 3 consecutive years may be asked to leave the network — ensuring a consistently high-performing community.

Debt Protection

A fund that has your back

PARTNERS UNLIMITED builds up a Debt Protection Fund to cover members for non-disputed, unpaid invoices of a PARTNERS UNLIMITED member. The Debt Protection Program is compulsory for all members.

Funds are built up by members on a yearly contribution basis. Details and application of the rules are defined in an Appendix.

  • Covers non-disputed unpaid invoices.
  • Compulsory for every member.
  • Funded by yearly member contributions.
