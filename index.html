Why Partners Unlimited

Built around people, protection & growth

Everything we do serves one purpose — to create real value for our members and forge a true partnership across borders.

Equal Acceptance

Regardless of market or company size, every member is treated equally and enjoys the same privileges within our network.

Debt Protection Fund

Our compulsory Debt Protection Program offers peace of mind by covering non-disputed unpaid invoices among members.

Regionalization

Organized into four regions, allowing for localized expertise while maintaining a truly global reach.

Annual Evaluation

An objective peer evaluation program keeps every member at a high standard of performance.

Shared Business

Members pledge to share business within the network, working toward an ultimate goal of 60% shared trade.

Our Core Value

People. People. People.

We believe the most important element is our members. The network is about connecting people and connecting needs — and the best ideas on how to operate come from the members themselves.

  • Equal footing — all members accepted and treated equally, regardless of size.
  • Value creation — the network must always create value for its members.
  • Member-driven — operations are shaped by the people who use them.
