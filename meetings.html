Meetings

Where partners meet & grow

Our Annual General Meeting and Global Sales Meeting bring members together to decide strategy, build relationships and create real business opportunities.

Flagship

Annual General Meeting

All senior management members are invited to attend. The AGM determines major issues and enriches the relationship among senior managers of the network.

  • Bilateral meetings presented among members.
  • Attendance encouraged at every meeting; mandatory once every 2 years.
  • Failure to attend consecutive meetings without good cause may be grounds for termination.
  • Meeting packages are all-inclusive except traveling cost.

Global Sales Meeting

For the sales professionals

The GSM is aimed at members' sales professionals — a platform to meet and develop sales strategy bilaterally. Meetings are part instructional (including real success cases) and part one-to-one bilateral meetings.

All members are encouraged to send their sales development team to the meeting.
