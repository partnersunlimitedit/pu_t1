Membership

A membership that pays for itself

Fair, tiered annual dues based on your market — with equal privileges, debt protection, and a global network included from day one.

Annual Dues Structure

Dues based on your country's market share

Annual membership dues are guided by the IMF/WTO market share of the member country. Dues for each category are set by the Board of Directors. Membership is on a yearly basis, renewable every 12 months.

Member Allocation

Limited seats per country

To keep the network exclusive and valuable, membership is capped per country. This ensures strong trade lanes and genuine partnership rather than internal competition.

  • Standard markets — maximum of 4 members per country.
  • China / USA / India — exception of up to 6 members.
  • Small markets — the Board may decide 1–2 members suffice.

Membership Terms

01
Payment terms

Dues payable within 30 days of invoice. Banking fees are on the account of the member.

02
Final acceptance

Acceptance is finalized only after membership dues are received.

03
Network rules

Payment of dues implies acceptance of the Network Rules and Ethics.

04
Shared risk

Transactions between members are subject to their own risk; the network is not held accountable.

05
Delinquency

Members should inform the Board of delinquent payments — recommended 60 days from invoice date.

Application for Membership

Qualified applicants may apply in writing or online to the Chairman of the Board. Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, you may be accepted into the network.
